Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel or modify my booking? ▼

You can cancel or modify most bookings up to 48 hours before your check-in date at no charge. For changes within 48 hours, please contact our support team directly via this contact form.

When will I receive my booking confirmation? ▼

Your booking is confirmed instantly and appears in your My Tickets page right away. You can also view all your trip details there at any time.

Are there any hidden fees? ▼

Never. The price you see on the booking page is the final price — it includes all taxes and fees. Our Best Price Promise means no surprises at checkout.

Can I book for a group? ▼

Absolutely! For groups of 6 or more, we offer special group rates and dedicated planning assistance. Please use the contact form above and select "Group Travel" as the subject.
